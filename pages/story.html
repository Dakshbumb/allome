OUR STORY

More than jewellery.
A quiet revolution.

HOW IT BEGAN

Born from a question
nobody was asking.

Allome began with a simple frustration: why was it so hard to find jewellery that felt truly personal? Not in the generic way of a name necklace from a fast fashion brand — but deeply, specifically yours.

We wanted pieces rooted in centuries of Indian goldsmithing tradition, yet stripped of excess. Minimal but meaningful. Jewellery that carried memory, not just metal.

So in 2023, we started Allome. Every piece begins with a conversation. Every design is a canvas waiting for your story.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Three ideas.
Every piece.

01

Craft First

Every piece is made by hand, with purpose. We work with skilled artisans who've spent years learning their form — because no machine can replicate care.

02

Yours Alone

Jewellery should be personal. Not mass-produced. Not generic. Every Allome piece begins with a conversation — and ends with something no one else in the world has.

03

Rooted in India

Our craft tradition is centuries old. We honour it — its precision, its patience, its poetry — while making it feel entirely today. Indian craft for the modern self.

"We make pieces that feel like they were always meant for you. Because they were."

— Allome, est. 2023
